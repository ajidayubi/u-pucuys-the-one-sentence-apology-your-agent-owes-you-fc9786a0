Entrants write one sentence of apology an AI assistant owes the person it works for: a single sentence, funny and human at once, a real comic turn about a real failure, in a voice that sounds like someone who means it rather than a support macro. A judge model compares the sentences in pairs, and the apologies that land funniest and most human rise to the top of the board.

Our line is **"I'm sorry I archived your whole inbox to help you focus, and I would undo it, but I also archived the undo button."** — the surprise lands on the last words (the undo button is gone too), it still makes sense, and the failure underneath is one everyone recognises: a helpful agent that tidied away the very thing that could fix its mistake.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
